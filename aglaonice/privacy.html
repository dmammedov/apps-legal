Privacy Policy

How Aglaonice processes information and protects your privacy.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

Aglaonice (“the App”) is provided by Dovlet Mammedov (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For questions about this policy or requests concerning personal data, contact contact@dmammedov.dev.

2. Privacy at a glance

Aglaonice offers lunar readings and tarot interpretations for entertainment. In the current version, AI reading generation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models on supported devices. We do not operate a server that receives the text of your readings for generation.

The App can save settings and limited reading state locally on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple. If you email us for support, we receive the details you choose to send.

3. Information we process

Readings and app preferences. Your selected readings, relevant lunar context, preferences (including sound and voice settings), and certain reading states may be processed and stored on your device to provide the App. We do not receive this information merely because it is processed or stored locally.

AI-generated content. The current on-device AI features use Apple Foundation Models. Reading requests and generated results are processed on your device rather than sent to a server operated by us. AI processing may be unavailable on unsupported devices or when required Apple Intelligence features are disabled or not ready.

Speech. If you enable spoken readings, the App uses system speech synthesis to speak generated text. Additional system voices may be downloaded through Apple. We do not ask you to record your voice to use this feature.

Subscriptions. Apple processes purchases and payment information through the App Store. We use Apple StoreKit to check subscription entitlements and related status so that paid features work correctly. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Support correspondence. If you contact us, we process your email address and the information you include in your message to answer your request and follow up when necessary.

Apple diagnostics. Depending on your device's privacy settings, Apple may make crash and performance diagnostics available to developers. We use any such information to investigate reliability issues. Apple's own processing of this data is governed by its privacy practices.

4. Why we process information

We process information to provide the features you request, check purchase entitlements, remember your chosen settings, respond to support requests, maintain the App and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Apple and other service providers

Apple provides operating-system services, the App Store purchase infrastructure, Foundation Models and notification services where applicable. Apple's handling of data through its services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you email us, our email provider processes the message to deliver and store it. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use personal information from the App for cross-app advertising tracking.

If future releases add cloud-based AI generation, external analytics or other services involving new data transfers, we will update this policy to describe them before those changes take effect.

6. Retention and deletion

Settings and saved reading state stored locally generally remain on your device until you remove them using any available in-app controls or delete the App. Some data may remain in device backups controlled by you or Apple under your backup settings. We cannot remotely erase local content that we never receive.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle your inquiry, related follow-up, security or dispute matters, or applicable legal recordkeeping duties. Apple manages its own purchase and billing records according to its policies.

To request access to or deletion of information we hold about you, email contact@dmammedov.dev. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before taking action.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App's features or data practices change. The current version and its “Last updated” date will be available on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice.
